Developed in conjunction with Ryan Knapton of Next Level Snowboarding, the Knapton twin-tip snowboard takes a freestyle snowboard and beefs it up to take carving and free-riding to the Next Level.

Built for aggressive soft-boot riders who want freestyle capability with serious carving performance. Every board is sized to your boots, weight and riding before it's laid up.
