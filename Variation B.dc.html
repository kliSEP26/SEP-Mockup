Where custom boards come to life

Whether you're laying down carves, racing gates, slashing pow, or catching air, a Donek Custom Snowboard is sure to upgrade your performance on the hill. Each Donek board is custom built based on the rider's needs and measurements, and you never have to compromise when it comes to aesthetics. Browse and compare our available board models and complete the form below to provide our engineers with information about you and your riding. Our team of experts will assist you with choosing the board, sizing, and specifications that are right for you. You can choose a topsheet and base graphic from our library, send in your custom artwork, or work with our in-house designer to create a one of a kind graphic that's sure to turn some heads on the mountain.
